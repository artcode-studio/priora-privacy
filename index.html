Privacy Policy  •  Effective Date: November 13, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information Priora collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data.

1. About This App

Priora is a daily planner and productivity app that helps you manage tasks, track goals, and stay focused using a Pomodoro timer. It works fully offline and optionally syncs data to the cloud when you sign in with Google.

  • Developer: Priora App
  • Package: com.planner.priora
  • Platform: Android
  • Contact: artcode.studio.business@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide

  • Task titles, descriptions, due dates, priorities, and categories
  • Sub-task content you create
  • Pomodoro session preferences

B. Google Account Information Optional

When you sign in with Google, we receive your:

  • Name and email address (for account identification only)
  • Google User ID (to associate your cloud data)

Signing in is entirely optional. The app works fully without an account (Guest Mode).

C. Usage & Crash Data

  • Crash reports and error logs (via Firebase Crashlytics) — anonymized stack traces
  • App usage events such as screens visited and features used (via Firebase Analytics)
  • Device type, OS version, and app version (automatically collected by Firebase)

D. Advertising Data

  • Advertising ID (Google Ad ID) — used by Google AdMob to serve relevant ads
  • IP address (collected by AdMob infrastructure for ad serving)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To store and display your tasks on your device
  • To sync your tasks across devices when you are signed in
  • To send local notifications for scheduled task reminders
  • To detect and fix app crashes and bugs
  • To understand how the app is used and improve it
  • To display advertisements through Google AdMob

4. Data Storage

Offline-First: All your tasks are stored locally on your device using an encrypted local database (Hive). Your data is yours — it never leaves your device unless you explicitly sign in with Google.
  • Local storage: Hive database on your device
  • Cloud storage (signed-in users only): Firebase Firestore, stored under your unique user ID at path users/{uid}/tasks/{taskId}
  • Signing out clears all local data from the device

5. Third-Party Services

Priora uses the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google LLC) — Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Crashlytics, Analytics
    firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Sign-In — Account authentication
    policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob — In-app advertising
    support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

6. Advertising

Priora displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your Advertising ID to show personalized ads based on your interests.

  • Ads appear as banners on the home screen and as full-screen interstitials
  • You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • We do not control the ads shown; AdMob's policies govern ad content

7. Notifications

Priora uses local notifications to remind you of scheduled tasks. These notifications are generated entirely on your device — no data is sent to external servers for notification delivery. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

8. Children's Privacy

Priora is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access your data: All task data is visible inside the app
  • Delete your data: Sign out of the app to clear all local data; contact us to delete cloud data permanently
  • Opt out of analytics: You can disable Firebase Analytics by contacting us or uninstalling the app
  • Opt out of personalized ads: Via Android device settings
  • Use without an account: Guest Mode keeps all data local only

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Firebase services use industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Firestore Security Rules ensure that only you can access your own data.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
